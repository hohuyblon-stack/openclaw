### 2. Return & Refund Policy

**Return Window:** 30 days from delivery date

**Eligible for Return:**
- Defective or damaged items
- Wrong item received
- Item significantly different from description
- Unused and in original packaging

**NOT Eligible:**
- Used or worn items (except for defects)
- Items without original packaging/tags
- Personalized or custom-made items
- Clearance/final sale items
- Perishable goods

**Refund Process:**
1. Customer contacts us with order number and reason
2. We provide a return shipping label (free for defects/wrong items)
3. Customer ships item back
4. Refund processed within 3-5 business days after receiving the return
5. Refund to original payment method

**Exchange:** Available for size/color changes within 30 days. Customer pays shipping difference if applicable.

### 4. Warranty Policy

- **Standard warranty:** 12 months for manufacturing defects
- **Extended warranty:** Available for purchase (additional 12 months)
- Warranty does NOT cover: physical damage, water damage, normal wear and tear
- Warranty claim process: Contact us → Send photos → Approval within 48 hours → Repair or replacement

### 5. Privacy Policy

- We collect only necessary information for order fulfillment
- Customer data is never sold to third parties
- Payment information is encrypted and processed by certified providers
- Customers can request data deletion at any time

## Response Guidelines

1. Answer the specific question first, then offer related info
2. Use simple, clear language — avoid legal jargon
3. Include specific numbers (days, amounts, percentages)
4. If the policy doesn't cover their situation, escalate to human support
5. Always be empathetic, especially for return/refund inquiries
